New to Cutaway? The apps aren't signed yet, so the first launch takes one extra step. **Mac:** unzip, move Cutaway.app to Applications and open it; when macOS refuses, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**. **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run Cutaway.exe; when Windows says it "protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Settings, shows and recordings carry over.

## Before you update: the remote now always has a PIN

The phone remote, the Stream Deck, OSC and anything else that talks to Cutaway's remote control now always need a PIN. If yours didn't have one, Cutaway makes a 4-digit PIN the first time it starts and shows it at the bottom of the window for half a minute; it stays in **Settings → Remote & MIDI → Remote control**. Type it into your phone's remote page and into the Stream Deck plugin's settings (**PIN**). After 5 wrong PINs in a row, that device has to wait a minute before it can try again.

## New in 0.9.0

**Building your scenes**
- **Fill, crop and zoom for each scene.** Each frame can show the whole picture or fill its box with no black bars. You can zoom in and move the picture around inside the frame.
- **Undo and redo** (Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z) for moving and resizing frames, scenes, titles and settings. Going live and recording are never undone.
- **A picker for screen capture.** It works like sharing your screen in a video call. Choose a whole screen, one window or one app. A window is found again after a restart, and comes back if you close it and open it again.
- **The second screen** lets you choose which display it goes on. It never covers Cutaway itself, and pressing Esc twice or clicking ✕ closes it.

**Graphics**
- **Animated titles.** Each graphic comes on with a fade, a slide, a wipe, a rise or a pop, and leaves the same way.
- **Web page overlays.** Put any web page or HTML file over the picture, with see-through parts left see-through. Good for StreamElements or Streamlabs alerts, chat boxes and scoreboards. It needs Chrome or Edge installed.
- **Graphics taking turns.** Give it a group of graphics, like sponsors or your social handles, and the next one comes on every few minutes. Every take's folder gets **Graphics on air.csv**, which says how long each graphic was on screen. That's handy for sponsor reports. Set it up in Settings → Graphics → Taking turns.

**Cameras and guests**
- **Blur or replace the background, no green screen.** In a source's Picture adjustments, you can blur the room, put a picture behind the people, or cut them out over another source.
- **Guest screen share.** Tick **Screen share** beside a guest's spot, and their page gets a Share screen button. Their screen arrives as its own source, separate from their camera.
- **Guest connection warnings.** Signal bars and a "Weak connection" or "Poor connection" tag show up before a guest's picture freezes. The guest also sees a hint on their own page about what might help.
- **Replay from any camera**, or every angle at once in a grid (`replay:Cam 2`, `replay:quad`). The Stream Deck's replay key has a new **From** setting for this.

**Sound**
- **Automatic mic mixing** for panels and round tables. Whoever is talking comes up, and the other mics go down. Set it up in Settings → Mixing & headphones.

**Running the show**
- **Your own keyboard shortcuts.** Move any built-in key or add your own in **Settings → General → Shortcuts**. Go Live has no key unless you give it one.
- **Scheduled streams.** Set up a YouTube event ahead of time with a title, a description and a thumbnail. Cutaway can go live by itself when the time comes. Set it up in Settings → Streaming → Scheduled stream. If you signed in to YouTube before, sign in once more so Cutaway can make the event.
- **Clearer waiting screens.** Tiles say "Connecting…" while a camera starts, and GO LIVE pulses while the stream connects. After you stop recording, you'll see each file as it saves. When a take is repaired after a crash, that happens in the background so you can carry on.

**Fixes**
- A full check of the app fixed rare crashes, places where work could be lost, and parts of the app that disagreed with each other. Settings are now saved in a way that can't leave a half-written file.

**Stream Deck plugin:** install the new plugin to get the replay key's **From** setting, and enter your remote PIN in its settings.
