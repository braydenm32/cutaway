New to Cutaway? The apps aren't signed yet, so the first launch takes one extra step. **Mac:** unzip, move Cutaway.app to Applications and open it; when macOS refuses, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**. **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run Cutaway.exe; when Windows says it "protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Settings, shows and recordings carry over.

## New in 0.9.8: a calmer Cutaway

**One window, a few areas**
- **Everything lives in the main window now.** A rail down the left has a handful of areas: **Show**, **Sources**, **Scenes**, **Sound**, **Graphics**, **Record** and **More**. The separate Settings window is gone; each setting sits in the area it belongs to.
- **Cutaway always opens on Show**, the screen you run the show from.
- **Find anything with ⌘K / Ctrl+K.** Type a few letters to jump to any setting, page or action.
- **Less used things are under More**: guests, captions, the rundown, controllers, the phone remote and app settings. A blue dot on More means a new version is out.

**Changes save as you go**
- **No more Apply or OK.** A change takes effect as you make it. **Undo** and **Redo** step back through your changes, and **Undo changes on this page** puts a whole page back.
- **The few changes that restart something** (adding or removing a source, the video size, a new audio input) wait on a bar at the bottom, such as **Apply (restarts cameras)**, so nothing restarts under you mid-show.

**During the show**
- **Clicking a multiview tile picks it, it doesn't put it on air.** Shift-click (or the scene keys, TAKE and CUT) still cuts straight to it.
- **Hold to stop works.** Holding the REC pill now really stops the recording, on the desktop and on the phone remote.
- **A steadier phone remote.** Scene taps no longer go missing, and it no longer says LIVE when every stream destination has failed.
- **The header fits** on small and mid-size screens, and the disk line isn't cut off.

**After the show**
- **Open in Resolve builds the project for you.** Cutaway sets up its Resolve script, opens Resolve, and you run **Workspace › Scripts › Cutaway - Build project from a take**. You get a timeline with your live edit on V1, every camera and the program full length on the tracks above it, the mix on A1, and your markers. If Resolve can't build it that way, it imports the .fcpxml instead, which now also stacks the cameras on their own tracks.
- **Takes have their own page** under Record › Takes, with the same Show in Finder/Explorer, Open in Resolve and Upload buttons.

**Fixes**
- Changing a setting while the show is running no longer undoes fader and input changes you made at the desk.
- **Name titles are easier to find.** Graphics › Titles & overlays shows who's named and has **＋ Make a name title** first.
- The Stream Deck Go Live key says GOING LIVE… and LIVE 1 of 2 like the phone, and OSC keeps sending the stream state while the window is minimised.
- Panels fit at 1280×800 and 1024×700.
