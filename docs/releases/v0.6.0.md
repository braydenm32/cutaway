## Download

| For | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13 or newer | **Cutaway-0.6.0-macos-arm64.zip** |
| Windows 11, 64-bit | **Cutaway-0.6.0-windows-x64.zip** |
| Stream Deck (Mac or Windows) | **Cutaway.streamDeckPlugin** |

The apps aren't signed yet, so the first launch takes one extra step. **Mac:** unzip, move Cutaway.app to Applications and open it; when macOS refuses, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**. **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run Cutaway.exe; when Windows says it "protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

From this version on, Cutaway tells you in its header when a newer one is out (it asks GitHub once at start; turn that off in Settings → General & shows). Settings, shows and recordings carry over when you update.

## New in 0.6.0

**Production**
- Stinger transitions (track matte, black-is-see-through or solid), fade to black (picture and sound), instant replay with slow motion, macros (commands with waits), and instant clips of the last few seconds.
- Clocks, countdowns and stopwatches; a rundown with segment timing, cues and chapters; a scrolling ticker; viewer comments from Twitch and YouTube chat on screen; a teleprompter on the second screen or any tablet.
- PTZ cameras over VISCA (IP, TCP, UDP) and NDI, and virtual PTZ inside a wide camera, with presets.
- Colour LUTs (.cube) per source.
- A vertical (9:16) version of the show with its own layout per scene, recorded and/or streamed to destinations marked vertical.

**Guests and cameras**
- Remote guests by link: they join from their browser, hear the show without their own voice (mix-minus) and your talkback, and can see the program. Optional backup recordings from their browser land in the take.
- Phone cameras: scan a QR code and a phone is a camera, up to 1080p over your Wi-Fi.

**Sound**
- Sound pads (once, loop, while held) on buttons, F-keys, the Stream Deck, MIDI and the remote; Syrinscape control; capturing one app's sound.
- Talkback, mix-minus outputs for callers, auto-ducking, and noise reduction (RNNoise) per input.

**Outputs and after the show**
- NDI output of the program and sources.
- Adaptive stream bitrate when a connection struggles.
- Upload each take to YouTube (with chapters) and/or Google Drive with your own Google sign-in.
- REAPER and DAWproject sessions (Fender Studio Pro, Bitwig, Cubase), and a DaVinci Resolve script that builds the whole project.

**Control**
- New Stream Deck keys: sound pads, Syrinscape, talkback, clips, timers, the rundown, guests, fade to black, replay, macros, PTZ, the teleprompter and viewer comments. The phone remote has them all too.

**Changed defaults:** ducking, guests hearing talkback, guests seeing the program, session files and rundown markers start off; each is one switch in Settings.

The full guide is in the [README](https://github.com/braydenm32/cutaway#readme).
