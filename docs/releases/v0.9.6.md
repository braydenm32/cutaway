New to Cutaway? The apps aren't signed yet, so the first launch takes one extra step. **Mac:** unzip, move Cutaway.app to Applications and open it; when macOS refuses, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**. **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run Cutaway.exe; when Windows says it "protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Settings, shows and recordings carry over.

## New in 0.9.6

**Settings**
- **Move and resize Settings.** Drag the window by its title and drag an edge or corner to resize it. It opens where you left it, and **Centre** puts it back. While the pointer is over the main window, the show keys (Space, Enter, scene numbers) keep working beside it.
- **See it before you apply it.** Each video source shows its picture above its settings. Crop and mirror show the result as you change them.
- **A tidier Type list.** Source types are grouped (cameras and screens, close-ups, network, other), and SRT and RTMP share one **Network feed** entry.
- **Viewer comments** appears under Show once you've added a stream destination, so a show that only records has one page fewer.

**Close-ups from one camera**
- **Aim a virtual PTZ by its boxes.** A virtual PTZ camera's settings show the wide picture with each preset as a box: drag it to move, scroll to zoom, and see what it sends out beside it. It now starts on preset 1.
- **Split a camera into close-ups.** Right-click a camera's tile and choose **Split into 2 (or 3) close-ups**. Each person gets their own virtual PTZ camera with a name title and its own recording, sized so half a 4K camera is a sharp 1080p close-up.
- **Lighter close-ups.** Cutting a close-up out of a camera takes much less of the processor: about 5x less for a 4K camera split in two.

**Main window**
- **A wider right-hand column.** Drag its left edge (the three dots) to make it wider or narrower. Cutaway remembers the width.

**Fixes**
- Changing something that restarts a camera while recording or live now asks first, and changing only a name title no longer restarts it.
- Settings undo can no longer switch shows or remove sources while recording or live.
- A virtual PTZ or auto-framed close-up now stays in sync when its camera has a delay.
- If the settings file can't be read, Cutaway says so, keeps a copy and offers fresh settings, instead of closing without a word.
- The remote's PIN no longer appears in the log or in problem reports.
- Instant clips of a ProRes program stay within a fixed amount of memory.
- Guest links keep reconnecting after a long show, instead of giving up.
