Settings, shows and recordings carry over.

## New in 0.9.10: lighter, calmer, easier

This is the last check before 1.0. Most of it you won't see: Cutaway does less work when nothing needs it, so a long show leaves more room for your cameras, and a laptop stays cooler.

**Lighter on your computer**
- **Close-ups can be saved as crops of the wide camera.** When one camera films two people and each gets a close-up, Cutaway used to save three videos of the same picture. Now each close-up can be saved **as a crop of Cam 1** instead (Sources › the close-up › Record). Your editor timeline still has every angle, framed the same, and you can reframe it later. The default is unchanged: each close-up keeps its own file until you choose otherwise. Close-ups that follow people always keep their own file.
- **Monitors are only drawn at full speed while someone is looking.** On a Settings page, with the window minimised, or deep in a long recording, Cutaway makes the monitor pictures once a second instead of thirty times. The second screen, the phone and the Stream Deck still get theirs at full speed.
- **A still screen or a paused video isn't copied over and over**, and picture scaling uses a few long-lived helpers instead of starting new ones for every frame.
- **On Windows, screen capture is converted on the graphics card**, so a moving screen (video, scrolling slides) uses much less of the processor. If a graphics card can't do it, Cutaway quietly uses the old way.
- **Fewer things wake up while idle:** auto switching, replays, stingers, guests and Syrinscape sleep until they're used.
- **A smaller, faster download**, built with stronger optimisation.

**Smoother to use**
- **Settings opens without a pause.** Cameras, mics and outputs are listed in the background ("Looking for devices…"), and choosing a file no longer freezes the window.
- **Saving happens in the background**, so typing in Settings stays smooth on big shows.
- **The first-show guide closes itself** once its steps are done, and has a new last step: **Switch from a phone or Stream Deck**.
- **Edit its layout ›** is one button on every scene. It shows the scene in preview while you edit, then puts things back.
- **Make a name title** with nobody named yet takes you straight to **Sources › People & names**.
- **The status light stays calm at rest**, and opening it marks the warnings as seen.
- **The Recording saved card stays 90 seconds** once Open in Resolve has shown its next step.
- **Plainer words** on screen and in the help ("Close-up you aim", "Close-up that follows people", dB instead of dBFS), and the manual now matches the app's names everywhere.

**Safer shows**
- **Only one Cutaway runs at a time.** A second double-click just tells you it's already open, so it can never touch a recording in progress.
- **The guest tunnel closes when you quit.**
- **A part that fails mid-recording says so straight away** in the health panel, instead of only at Stop.
- **Settings are saved first when you quit**, and quitting no longer waits for a podcast copy.
- **A camera that comes back after dropping out** picks up smoothly in its file, without a burst of catch-up frames.
- **A phone that joins late keeps all its sound**, without holding it all in memory.
- **An RTMP sender that drops off Wi-Fi can reconnect**, and one that stops normally no longer reports an error.

**Phone remote and Stream Deck**
- **Nothing gets stuck held.** If the phone or Stream Deck goes quiet while you hold talkback, a pad or a camera move, Cutaway lets go by itself within 3 seconds.
- **The Stream Deck reconnects by itself** when Cutaway restarts or the connection drops.
- **The phone shows "Cutaway isn't updating"** if the desk stops answering, and a tapped button dims while it waits.
- **Lighter on the phone's battery and Wi-Fi:** the picture only updates when it changes, clocks tick in whole seconds, and a hidden page stops asking.
- **Keep screen on starts off** on the phone remote (turn it on if you want it; your choice is remembered). Tally pages always keep the screen on.
