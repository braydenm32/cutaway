**Already on 0.8.0?** Use **Update now** in the header's notice; you don't need to download anything.

New to Cutaway? The apps aren't signed yet, so the first launch takes one extra step. **Mac:** unzip, move Cutaway.app to Applications and open it; when macOS refuses, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**. **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run Cutaway.exe; when Windows says it "protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Settings, shows and recordings carry over.

## New in 0.8.1

**A new look**
- Cutaway has a logo: a leaning C and A, the A's left leg a red cut, inside a camera's corner brackets with a red recording light.
- On a Mac, Cutaway.app has an icon at last (it was a blank one), in Finder, Launchpad and the Dock.
- On Windows, Cutaway.exe, the taskbar and the window's title bar all show it.
- The phone remote and teleprompter pages show it in the browser tab, and on your home screen if you add them there.
- The Stream Deck plugin uses it too. Installing the new plugin is optional: only its icons changed.

**About Cutaway**
- Click the logo at the top left to see which version you have, with links to what's new and to the guide.

**Mac: still seeing a blank icon?** macOS remembers app icons. If the Dock or Finder keeps showing the old blank one after the update, take Cutaway out of the Dock and drag it back in from Applications, or restart the Mac.

The full guide is in the [README](https://github.com/braydenm32/cutaway#readme).
