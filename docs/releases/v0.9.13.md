**Already on 0.9?** Use **Update now** in the update notice; you don't need to download anything. The Stream Deck plugin hasn't changed apart from its version number, so there's no need to reinstall it.

New to Cutaway? Start with **[Your first hour](https://github.com/braydenm32/cutaway/blob/v0.9.13/docs/guide/first-hour.md)**. **Mac:** unzip, move Cutaway.app to Applications and open it. **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run Cutaway.exe; the Windows app isn't signed yet, so when Windows says it "protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Settings, shows and recordings carry over.

## New in 0.9.13: identical cameras, and finer control in Edit layout

**Several of the same camera**
- **Each camera shows its own picture.** Until now, if you had two or three cameras of the same make and model, every source showed the first one's picture. Cameras that share a name are now listed as "CamStream 4K", "CamStream 4K (2)", "CamStream 4K (3)", and each source remembers the exact camera you picked.
- **They're numbered by the USB port they're plugged into.** Keep each camera in the same port and it keeps its number. If you move one to another port, its source falls back to the camera with the same number, so check the pictures once before the show.
- **Existing shows:** a source you set up before this version still finds its camera by name. Pick the right camera for each one once in Sources, and from then on it remembers.

**Finer control in Edit layout**
- **Arrow keys move the picked frame** one pixel at a time (Shift + arrow: ten). With **✥ Move picture** on, or Option/Alt held, they move the picture inside the frame instead. A burst of presses is one step to undo.
- **Swap a source in one click.** Right-click a frame (or pick it and use **Frame › Swap for ▾**) and choose another source: it shows in the same place and size, keeping Fit or Fill, so *Cam 2 left* becomes *Cam 3 left*. A source that's already in the scene trades places with it, each picture keeping its own zoom.
- **TAKE and CUT move out of the way while you edit.** They now sit in the bar at the top beside **PREVIEW**, instead of over the picture's bottom-right corner, where they covered the resize handle of the right-hand frame.
