**Already on 0.9?** Use **Update now** in the header's notice; you don't need to download anything.

New to Cutaway? The apps aren't signed yet, so the first launch takes one extra step. **Mac:** unzip, move Cutaway.app to Applications and open it; when macOS refuses, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**. **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run Cutaway.exe; when Windows says it "protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Settings, shows and recordings carry over.

## New in 0.9.7

**During the show**
- **Hold to stop.** Stopping a recording means holding the REC pill for a moment, and Cmd+R / Ctrl+R asks first. On the phone remote you hold the button too. One stray click or pocket tap can't end a take any more.
- **Named markers.** Shift+M adds a marker and lets you type a name for it. A list of this take's markers sits beside the Marker button.
- **When a source drops, you'll know.** If a source on air loses its signal, the program picture says so and its scene buttons get a ⚠. You can choose a scene to cut to automatically when that source drops.
- **Back to the last scene.** Backspace (or the "back" command on a controller) goes back to the scene you were just on.
- **A better scene bank.** Each scene button shows a live picture of its scene and its full name on hover. Arrows show when there are more scenes off-screen, and a tall window gets a second row.
- **A clock in the header**, in 12 or 24 hour time.
- **Set up streaming.** Until you add a stream destination, GO LIVE becomes a smaller **Set up streaming** button, so it can't be pressed by mistake.
- **Edit the scene in preview.** With Preview on, Edit layout changes the scene in preview, not the one on air. It also says when there are unsaved changes.

**Scenes and setup**
- **Make scenes properly.** Edit layout has **＋ New scene** (blank, or from a layout), and you can drag scene buttons into a new order. Settings → Show → Scenes lists them all with their number keys, to reorder, rename and delete.
- **Split makes a "both" scene.** Splitting a camera into close-ups also adds a scene with them side by side.
- **Audio inputs** show a live level bar on each row, and can be reordered and duplicated.
- **The same list buttons everywhere:** ＋ Add, Duplicate, Remove, ↑ and ↓ work the same for sources, audio inputs, graphics, destinations, rundown and macros.
- **Find a setting.** Type in the box above the page list to jump straight to any setting.
- **QR code for the phone remote.** Scan it with your phone's camera to open the remote with the PIN already in.
- **A fuller first-show guide.** Name each camera and who's in it right in the guide, then set up your scenes and editing afterwards.
- **Clearer name-title buttons**, and a hint when no source has a name yet.

**After the show**
- **"Take saved" card.** When a take finishes saving, a card under Record shows its length and size, with buttons to show it in Finder or Explorer, open it in Resolve or upload it.
- **Recent takes.** The ▾ beside Record lists your last ten takes, with the same buttons.
- **Takes named after the show.** If the take name is left as "Take", the folder uses the show's name.
- **Uploads carry on after quitting.** Unfinished uploads pick up where they left off next time you open Cutaway.
- **Dates come from when the take started**, so a show past midnight or uploaded the next day gets the right date.
- **Chapters follow YouTube's rules**, and ✂ Clip markers are no longer turned into chapters.
- **Resolve:** installing the script also turns on "a project from each take". A take folder you've moved still imports, and the timeline matches the show's size (4K or vertical).
- **Uploads wait for the podcast file**, so it goes up too.

**Look**
- **Red only means on air.** REC is a dark pill with a pulsing red dot, and TAKE, CUT and the like have their own colours.
- **Tidier and easier to read.** State text is bigger, header buttons share one style, meters fill their column, and text sizes are consistent everywhere.
- **Settings' own title bar.** Drag it to move the window and double-click it to centre. Revert is on the left, and OK only appears when there's something to apply.

**Fixes**
- Errors stay on screen until you close them, and they're written to the Log. Low disk space warnings show on screen too.
- Log (n) counts only new warnings since you last looked. The Log has **Warnings only** and **Copy**.
- A muted mic says **OFF**.
- The header says **Typing: show keys off (Esc)** while a text box has the keyboard.
- Starting from the phone, Stream Deck, MIDI or OSC runs the pre-show check too, when it's turned on.
- Quitting or restarting to update waits while a take, podcast or upload is still finishing.
- Desk changes (faders, inputs, transition, layout) are saved as you go, so a crash doesn't lose them.
- Switching shows from "No show" saves your setup as "My setup" first.
- Deleting a show asks first, and saving over one says so.
- The main window remembers its size and place, and fits a small screen the first time.
- The time left on the disk is the same figure everywhere, and the pre-show check gives a clear verdict.
- Settings catches duplicate audio input names and close-ups with no camera, and the error links to the setting.
- A new mic input is named after its device.
- Cancelling the logo picker no longer adds an empty logo.
- The teleprompter and tally pages keep the phone awake.
- A scene name typed just before Apply or OK is kept.
