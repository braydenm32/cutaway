New to Cutaway? Start with **[Your first hour](https://github.com/braydenm32/cutaway/blob/v0.9.9/docs/guide/first-hour.md)**. The apps aren't signed yet, so the first launch takes one extra step. **Mac:** unzip, move Cutaway.app to Applications and open it; when macOS refuses, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**. **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run Cutaway.exe; when Windows says it "protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Settings, shows and recordings carry over.

## New in 0.9.9: safer shows, and recordings you can't lose

**Icons show up again.** Plus signs, clocks, ticks and crosses showed as empty boxes on some Macs. Cutaway now ships every symbol it uses.

**Harder to make a mistake on air**
- **Hold to confirm, everywhere.** Fade to black, turning a mic off while you're recording or live, and disconnecting a guest take a one-second hold, on the desk, the phone remote and the Stream Deck. Coming back from black and turning a mic on are still one click.
- **Recording is unmistakable.** The REC pill turns solid red with a pulsing dot while you record, and a red **● REC** chip sits on the program picture, where your eyes are.
- **"No sound" warnings.** If the recording is silent (a mic off, muted or unplugged), a small **no sound** shows beside REC within seconds, and after 45 seconds you get a clear alert, on the phone too.
- **Errors stay put.** An error message stays for 30 seconds or until you click it, and several stack up instead of replacing each other.
- **Undo puts back only what you changed**, and a change on a settings page no longer touches faders or inputs you moved on the desk.

**Recordings you can't lose**
- **Crash rescue brings everything back.** If Cutaway or the computer stops mid-show, the rescued recording gets its live edit, markers, chapters, proxies and timecode back, and Open in Resolve builds it as usual.
- **Files are put on disk every few seconds**, and a file whose finish failed is mended.
- **A camera that joins or is replugged mid-show** gets its own file straight away, lined up with the rest.
- **Recordings lists every folder** you've recorded to, not just the current one.
- **Warnings before you start:** a FAT32 drive (which stops at 4 GB), a show too big for the computer, and a drive that's filling up.

**Clearer names**
- A recording is called a recording everywhere: **Record & stream › Recordings**, the **Recording saved** card, **Last recording**. "Take" now only means sending the preview to air.
- The **Record** area is **Record & stream**, and its settings tab is **Quality and files**.

**Phone remote and Stream Deck**
- **The phone** has fixed TAKE and CUT buttons, a Preview switch, hold a scene to cut straight to air, quick marker names, keeps its screen on, shows **Reconnecting…** when it loses touch, and answers the pre-show check.
- **The Stream Deck** has Preview and Back keys, scene keys that behave like the desk's, a Record key that shows when something needs a look and when a recording is saving, and the hold for fade to black and mics.
- Wrong PINs are slowed down after a few tries, and held keys can't get stuck.

**Streaming**
- **Stream failures in plain words**, such as a stream key the service refuses, with a button to the place to fix it.
- If the graphics card's encoder fails, Cutaway falls back to another one, and **Test encoders** checks them all.

**Setting up**
- **＋ Add** lists every kind of source in one menu.
- The first-show guide has a **Record a test** button, waits for macOS's permission questions, and a dark camera tile says why it's dark.
- **0** takes the tenth scene, the Shortcuts page shows clashing keys, and on a MacBook Cutaway reminds you to hold Fn for the sound pad keys.
- On the Mac download the virtual camera is hidden with a "coming later" note, instead of a button that can't work.

**On the desk**
- Two people on one camera get both names on the title ("Name & Name").
- A message when a remote guest joins or leaves.
- The STREAM meter shows only when you're streaming.
- Edit layout works on the scene in preview mid-show.
- Panel headings fold from their arrow or name and drag only from the grip.

**After the show**
- **The Recording saved card hides itself** after 20 seconds when all went well. Shift+M names a marker in a small box instead of a dialog.
- **Resolve:** running the script on two recordings of the same show on the same day offers **Add to this project**.

**Signed updates**
- From this version on, Cutaway only installs an update that's signed by its maker, on top of checking its fingerprints, so a tampered download can't get in.

**A proper manual**
- The manual now has one page per part of the show, plus **Your first hour**: requirements, permissions, where your files go, what Cutaway sends over the internet, and the common first-show problems. **More › App › Help** and **About** open it.
