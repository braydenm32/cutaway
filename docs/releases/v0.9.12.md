New to Cutaway? Start with **[Your first hour](https://github.com/braydenm32/cutaway/blob/v0.9.12/docs/guide/first-hour.md)**. **Mac:** unzip, move Cutaway.app to Applications and open it. **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run Cutaway.exe; the Windows app isn't signed yet, so when Windows says it "protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## New in 0.9.12: the Mac app is signed, and the virtual camera comes to the Mac

**Signed and checked by Apple**
- **Cutaway for Mac now opens like any other app.** It's signed with our Apple Developer ID and checked by Apple (notarized), so there's no more "Apple could not verify Cutaway" and no trip to Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
- **Updates stop asking for your Keychain.** Until now each update looked like a new app to macOS, so it asked again to use the stream keys Cutaway keeps in your Keychain. **Once more after this update**, choose **Always Allow**, and you may need to allow the camera, microphone and screen recording once more too. After that, updates are quiet.
- The Windows app isn't signed yet; that's coming.

**Cutaway as a webcam on the Mac**
- **The virtual camera is in the Mac download.** Put Cutaway in Applications, choose **Record & stream › Screens & webcam › Install…** once, then press **Webcam** under the status light and pick the **Cutaway** camera in Zoom, Teams, Meet or FaceTime. See [Virtual camera](https://github.com/braydenm32/cutaway/blob/v0.9.12/docs/guide/install.md#virtual-camera).

## Also new since 0.9.10: ready-made layouts, and a safer, more private Cutaway

0.9.11 wasn't released on its own, so its changes come with this version.


**Ready-made layouts for your cameras**
- **A new show starts with the layouts it has cameras for**, after each source on its own: **2 cameras** side by side, **3 cameras** across, a **2 × 2 grid** for 4, **5 cameras** in equal tiles (two each side, the first camera in the middle), **Screen and cameras** (your screen large, up to eight cameras in a row below) and **Picture in picture**. They replace the old Side by side and Grid.
- **Cutaway fills the tiles for you.** Cameras and close-ups go in in order, a wide camera that close-ups are cut from makes room for its close-ups, and a screen capture (or a guest's shared screen) goes in the screen tile. Pictures are cropped to fill their tiles, and you can still move and zoom each crop in Edit layout.
- **Reset to default** (in Edit layout, or under the scene in Scenes › Scenes) puts a layout scene back the way it started, using today's sources, and keeps its name, sound and cues. Undo brings your version back.
- **Add default scenes** (Scenes › Scenes) adds any of these layouts your show is missing, without touching your other scenes.
- **＋ New scene › From a layout** lists them all. One that needs more cameras than you have is greyed out and says what it needs.
- **Your existing shows aren't changed.**

**Safer**

We checked Cutaway from top to bottom for anything someone could misuse, and fixed what we found. Most of it you'll never see. The parts you might notice:
- **The phone remote is harder to get into.** New PINs have six digits, and other web sites open in the same phone's browser can't talk to Cutaway.
- **RTMP inputs get long, random stream keys** for new inputs.
- **Twitch uses its secure (RTMPS) server** when you add a new Twitch destination, so your stream key travels encrypted, as YouTube's already does. Destinations you've already set up keep their server.
- **SRT feeds and outputs can have a passphrase.** Only senders who know it get in, and the picture is encrypted on the way.
- **OSC listens to this computer only**, unless you tick **From other devices** (More › Control › OSC and MIDI). If you already use OSC from another device, that keeps working as it did.
- **Remote guests get their own short-lived relay login** each, and the guest page tells them before they join that the show may be recorded or streamed.
- **Settings files you import are safer.** Cutaway shows what a file will change, stream destinations come in switched off, and an exported file leaves out your phone remote PIN.
- **Damaged or odd files can't trip Cutaway up**: video, sound and timeline files with broken sizes, names that would confuse a spreadsheet or Windows, and links inside a recording's folder are all handled safely.
- **The updater only touches Cutaway's own files**, the downloads Cutaway fetches (the guest tunnel and speech models) are checked against known fingerprints, and problem reports hide more private details.

**More private**
- **Remove my settings and keys…** (More › App › Diagnostics) removes everything Cutaway keeps on your computer, including stream keys in the Keychain or Credential Manager. Your recordings stay.
- **A new [Privacy](https://github.com/braydenm32/cutaway/blob/v0.9.12/docs/guide/privacy.md) page** in the manual says plainly what Cutaway keeps, where, and what it sends (only what you set up). Logs are kept for a month at most.
